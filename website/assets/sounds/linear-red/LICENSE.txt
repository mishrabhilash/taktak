Changes made (by TakTak contributors, 2026-10-02)
-------------------------------------------------

These are modified excerpts of the original recording, not the recording itself:

- The two (identical) channels were mixed to mono and a 30 Hz high-pass filter was
  applied to remove DC and sub-audio rumble.
- 24 short excerpts (17-78 ms each) were cut out of the 17.7-second recording: 13 single
  letter keystrokes, 7 space-bar key-downs and 4 space-bar key-ups. Each excerpt starts
  0.5 ms before its keystroke, with a 0.5 ms fade-in, and ends with a 6-8 ms fade-out.
- One gain of +7.51 dB was applied to every excerpt, and a further -5.35 dB to the
  space-bar excerpts. The results were saved as 16-bit, 48 kHz mono WAV files.
- preview.wav is a short typing phrase mixed from these excerpts.
- pack.json plays the whole pack at a volume of 0.94 (-0.5 dB), for headroom and to
  match the loudness of the other packs (2026-10-03); the audio files are not changed
  by it.
- Which keystrokes were key-downs and which were key-ups was inferred from the timing of
  the recording; it was not recorded, so some of the letter excerpts may be key-ups.

SOURCES.md lists the exact sample range, fades and gain of every file, and
tools/pack-sources/linear-red/ in the TakTak repository rebuilds the pack from the
original file.
