TakTak टक-टक · the Hindi sound of tapping

Your keyboard, but it goes TakTak.

A tiny menu-bar app that plays real mechanical keyboard sounds as you type, in every app, about 6 ms after the key goes down. Free and open source for macOS, Windows and Linux.

The promise

Fully offline — TakTak never uses the internet.

No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports, no update checks, no downloads. There is no network code in the app at all, so it works the same with Wi-Fi off, forever.

Keyboard access, for one thing only

To click when you press a key in another app, TakTak needs the system's keyboard-monitoring permission (on macOS: Input Monitoring). It uses it only to know that a key went down or came back up, and which physical key, so it can play that key's sound. Revoke it any time and TakTak just goes quiet.

Never stored, logged or sent

TakTak never sees the characters you type and never reads your keyboard layout. It plays a sound and forgets the key. It keeps no log files, saves nothing but your settings, and has nowhere to send anything. Password fields and the lock screen are always silent on macOS.

Open source, so anyone can check

Every line is public under the MIT license. CI fails the build if a networking library, a network API or an outside URL ever shows up in the app. Don't take our word for it: read the privacy rules and the code.

This website is the same: no trackers, no cookies, no analytics, and no requests to anyone else's servers.

Features

Small app, real clicks

  • Real press and release

    Each key gets its own key-down and key-up sound where the recording has them, from 8 recorded packs: tactile switches to a typewriter.

  • About 6 ms latency

    A dedicated real-time audio path: 5.9 ms median from key-down to sound, measured on a MacBook Pro's own keyboard and speakers.

  • Open pack format

    A pack is a folder with a pack.json and some audio files. Drop it in and it appears within a second, no restart.

  • Import Mechvibes packs

    Convert your Mechvibes packs (v1, v2, DX, ++) for personal use. They play on your machine and are never bundled or shared.

  • Per-app rules

    Sounds everywhere, only in some apps, or never in some apps (macOS for now).

  • Auto-mute on lock

    Silent on the lock screen, during user switching and in password fields (macOS), and optionally when your headphones disconnect.

  • Lives in your menu bar

    A tray popover to switch packs, change the volume, mute and preview. Settings for press and release volume, "humanize" and a global mute hotkey.

  • Cross-platform

    macOS 11+ (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10/11 and Linux (X11 and Wayland). Light on the system: idle, it closes the audio device.

Make your own pack

Record your keyboard. Share it.

A pack is a folder (or a .zip) with a pack.json and some WAV, Ogg or MP3 files. Record your own board with pack-maker, or slice a recording you're allowed to use, then drop it in your packs folder: TakTak picks it up in about a second. A broken pack never breaks TakTak.

Adding a pack, step by step Pack format spec 
{
  "format": 1,
  "id": "my-board",
  "name": "My Board",
  "author": "You",
  "license": "CC0-1.0",
  "groups": {
    "alphanumeric": {
      "press":   ["sounds/down-1.wav", "sounds/down-2.wav"],
      "release": ["sounds/up-1.wav", "sounds/up-2.wav"]
    },
    "space": { "press": ["sounds/space.wav"] }
  }
}
